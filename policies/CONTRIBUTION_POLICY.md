# Contribution Policy

V1 is maintainer-led and scope-controlled.

Contributions must:

- target the supported stack;
- include tests and docs;
- preserve transaction and ownership invariants;
- avoid new production dependencies without rationale;
- pass clean-room generation;
- include license provenance for copied assets;
- avoid broad refactors unrelated to the contribution.

New stack adapters are not accepted during V1 stabilization.
